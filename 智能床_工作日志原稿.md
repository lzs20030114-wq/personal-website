## 2026-05-19｜首轮资料检索与竞品分析
**标签：#调研 Research / #竞品 Competitive analysis**

**CN** — 完成智能床项目首轮资料检索与竞品分析,确定四个市场空白作为设计切入点。

项目是清华未来实验室与慕思合作的2030智慧睡眠前瞻研究。梳理了四档竞品:Eight Sleep(控温)、Sleep Number与Bryte(气动调节)、慕思T11+(国产一体机)、小米华为生态低价产品,发现行业的共同问题是过度干预——用户抱怨床在人睡着后自行移动把人吵醒。据此提出四个空白:克制的智能(知道什么时候不介入)、家具感而非家电感的形态、把"关系"而非单人作为设计对象、把睡眠当作仪式而非待修复的问题。产出一份文献导读、一套18页竞品分析PPT和一张竞品功能对照图。

**EN — First Round of Research and Competitive Analysis**
May 19 — Completed the first round of research and competitive analysis for the smart bed project, landing on four market gaps as design entry points.

The project is a 2030 sleep-foresight study run jointly by Tsinghua's Future Lab and DeRucci, a Chinese premium mattress brand. I mapped four tiers of competitors — Eight Sleep (temperature control), Sleep Number and Bryte (pneumatic adjustment), DeRucci's T11+ (a domestic all-in-one), and budget ecosystem products from Xiaomi and Huawei — and found the industry shares one failure: over-intervention, with users complaining that the bed moves on its own at night and wakes them up. From this came four gaps: restrained intelligence (knowing when not to intervene), furniture-like rather than appliance-like form, designing for the relationship in the bed rather than a single optimized sleeper, and treating sleep as a ritual rather than a problem to fix. Deliverables: an annotated reading guide, an 18-page competitive analysis deck, and a feature comparison chart.

## 2026-06-01 至 06-05｜报告 3.3 节「未来典型睡眠人群假设」初稿
**标签：#写作 Writing / #文献 Literature**
> 站上条目按 schema 要求钉为单日 `2026-06-05`（区间末日 = 完成日），正文里说明「五天」。

**CN** — 写完报告3.3节"未来典型睡眠人群假设"完整初稿,定下"基础人群全集→四类演化"框架。

3.3是第三章的收口:把前两节盘点的现有人群按到2030年的演化方式分成四类(需求被放大、需求转向、需求显现、需求稳定)。写作中砍掉了几处站不住的内容:一个未能在一手来源核实的围绝经期数据整条删除;呼吸暂停风险人群因缺乏结构性放大的证据从放大型挪回稳定型;"近1.5亿空巢老人"补写限定,与"772万高龄独居"明确区分,防止混读。最终交付Word与PDF,含19条逐一核实的ACM格式参考文献;第一版彩色圆角框插图被换成黑白线稿的学术风格。

**EN — Section 3.3, "Hypotheses on Future Typical Sleep Populations"**
June 1–5 — Drafted the report's Section 3.3, "Hypotheses on Future Typical Sleep Populations," in full, built on a "full population inventory → four evolution types" frame.

Section 3.3 closes out the chapter: it takes the existing populations catalogued in the two preceding sections and sorts them by how they will evolve toward 2030 — needs amplified, needs redirected, needs surfacing into view, or needs staying stable. Several things were cut for failing the evidence bar: a perimenopause statistic went out entirely because it could not be verified at the primary source; the sleep-apnea-risk group moved from "amplified" back to "stable" for lack of evidence of structural growth; and the phrase "nearly 150 million empty-nest elderly" was qualified to keep it clearly separate from the much smaller "7.72 million living alone at advanced age," so readers cannot conflate the two. Final delivery was a Word and PDF manuscript with 19 individually verified ACM-format references; the first version's colorful rounded-box figures were replaced with black-and-white academic line drawings.

## 2026-06-04｜「舒适度数据跟人走」概念深化与中文展板
**标签：#概念设计 Concept design**

**CN** — 把访谈练习里的智能床概念深化成"舒适度数据跟人走"的系统方案,生成中文展板。

起点是解决"认床"问题的自适应床:读脉搏、体温、压力信号,自动调整形态和温度。深化后的框架是个人舒适度配置预先存好,像Apple ID同步设置一样,任何接入设备(不限于床)都能自动适配,价值在跨空间的可携带性。展板文案按"设计概述"的客观密实文体重写,替换掉前几稿偏抒情的口号式写法。

**EN — Developing the Portable-Comfort-Profile Concept, with a Chinese Board**
June 4 — Developed a quick interview-exercise bed concept into a system where personal comfort data travels with the person, and produced a Chinese presentation board.

The starting point was an adaptive bed for the problem Chinese speakers call renchuang — sleeping badly in any unfamiliar bed — reading pulse, temperature, and pressure to adjust its shape and warmth automatically. The developed frame goes further: a personal comfort profile is configured once and stored, and any connected device — not only beds — adapts to it automatically, the way an Apple ID syncs settings onto a new phone. The value is portability across spaces. The board copy was rewritten in the dense, objective register of a design brief, replacing earlier drafts that leaned lyrical and slogan-like.

**EN — Repairing the Merged Chapter and Building the Evolution Diagram**
June 12 — After the three-author chapter was merged, repaired broken cross-references and an inconsistent classification axis, and built an evolution diagram for Section 3.3.

My section cited an "11-category population list" from an earlier section that no longer existed after that section was restructured; tracing the broken link surfaced five orphaned population entries whose source had vanished, and they were cleaned out. The axis dividing the four evolution types was unified as "whether mainstream discourse already recognizes this group" — and the long-argued question of where solo-living young adults belong was settled by that axis: they go into the surfacing type. The section's closing summary was finalized as four judgments: demand growth concentrates where demographic shifts intersect; sleep is increasingly embedded in relationships and caregiving rather than being a solo behavior; some groups are turning sleep from a problem to fix into a resource to manage; and a set of long-overlooked populations is coming into view. The evolution diagram was done as an SVG wireframe that encodes each evolution type in the line itself — thickening for amplification, an elbow bend for redirection, dashed-to-solid for surfacing — and needed two rounds of geometry fixes to stop lines crossing through circles and labels. ## 2026-06-30｜倒班人群与独居青年：证据审计与画像手稿
**标签：#画像 Personas / #文献 Literature**

**CN** — 完成倒班人群与独居青年两组的证据审计和画像手稿,期间推翻了两个沿用的画像范式。

先做深度证据审计(中文一手来源优先,企业自报数据单独标注并降级,外推结论显式标记),确认证据够用才动笔。写作中放弃了具名个体式画像卡("林梅,29岁,广州急诊护士"这类),读起来刻板别扭,改为"这类人通常是什么样"的类型级描写;也放弃了参考模板的2×2定位矩阵——职业定义的人群套不进两个连续轴——换成单轴光谱(倒班组:作息可预测↔不可预测;独居组:主动熬夜↔被动失眠)。两份手稿各含定位段、光谱、四组跨类型需求(对应RU-SATED睡眠健康六维度)、两个类型画像和文末证据清单。此期定下一条持续沿用的规则:不造新词,只用项目和文献里已有的词汇。

**EN — Evidence Audits and Persona Manuscripts for Two Groups**
June 30 — Completed evidence audits and persona manuscripts for two groups — shift workers and solo-living young adults — and threw out two inherited persona conventions along the way.

A deep evidence audit came first (Chinese primary sources prioritized, company self-reported data labeled and downgraded, extrapolations flagged explicitly); writing only began once the evidence held. Two conventions did not survive contact with the material. Named-individual persona cards ("Lin Mei, 29, ER nurse in Guangzhou") read as stereotyped and awkward, so they were replaced with type-level profiles — "what this kind of person is typically like." And the reference template's 2×2 positioning matrix would not fit occupationally defined groups, which cannot vary along two continuous axes, so it became a single-axis spectrum instead: predictable-to-unpredictable schedule for shift workers, deliberate bedtime delay to involuntary insomnia for solo-living youth. Each manuscript contains a positioning paragraph, the spectrum, four cross-type need clusters mapped to RU-SATED (a six-dimension sleep-health framework: regularity, satisfaction, alertness, timing, efficiency, duration), two type profiles, and an end-of-document evidence list. One rule set here has held since: coin no new vocabulary — use only terms already in the project or the source literature.

## 2026-07-07｜画像换人、独居青年解散、五张画像卡与筛选题组
**标签：#画像 Personas / #问卷 Survey**

**CN** — 换掉不成立的骑手画像,解散独居青年独立人群,产出五张画像卡和一套筛选题组。

外卖骑手被组里否掉:骑手可自选工时,不符合"作息被工作打乱"那一端。推理后换成平台货运卡车司机——接单后任务锁死、贷款压力不敢休息、城市限行、待命状态只能浅睡。独居青年的问题更根本:独居本身不直接产生睡眠需求,只放大别处产生的需求,于是拆掉这个人群,内容分流进三个宿主人群(主动熬夜→高压白领,作息漂移→不规律作息,孤独入睡难→睡眠障碍倾向),另立一节"独居"作为横切变量。可视化产出五张JPG画像卡,五格睡眠阶段分镜加RU-SATED受损雷达(标注为判断编码、非实测)。筛选问卷约18题,靠变量组合而非受访者自选身份来识别全部已定义人群。

**EN — Replacing a Persona, Dissolving a Population, Five Cards and a Screener**
July 7 — Replaced a persona that didn't hold up, dissolved solo-living youth as a standalone population, and produced five persona cards plus a screener question set.

The delivery-rider persona was rejected by the team, and rightly: riders can choose their own hours, so they don't belong at the "schedule disrupted by work" end of the spectrum. Reasoning through alternatives landed on platform freight truck drivers — once a shipment is accepted the task is locked, loan payments make rest feel unaffordable, city traffic restrictions dictate driving windows, and standby waiting permits only shallow sleep. Solo-living youth had a deeper problem: living alone generates no sleep needs of its own, it only amplifies needs generated elsewhere. The population was dismantled and its content redistributed into three host populations (deliberate bedtime delay → high-pressure office workers; schedule drift → irregular sleepers; loneliness-driven trouble falling asleep → those with disorder tendencies), with "living alone" kept as a cross-cutting variable in its own section. On the visual side, five JPG persona cards were produced, each a five-panel storyboard across the stages of a night plus a radar chart of RU-SATED damage, labeled as encoded judgment rather than measurement. The screener runs about 18 items and identifies every defined population through combinations of variables, not by asking respondents to self-select a label.

## 2026-07-09 至 07-14｜运动员访谈提纲 v2（33 题 / 60 分钟）
**标签：#访谈 Interviews**
> 站上条目按 schema 要求钉为单日 `2026-07-14`（区间末日 = 完成日）。

**CN** — 为体育部教师(前全国比赛游泳运动员)访谈写出v2完整提纲:33题,60分钟。

先查方法论(The Mom Test、Portigal的《洞察用户》、关键事件技术)和已发表的运动员睡眠质性研究,再评审自己旧稿与队友平行稿,两稿的共同问题是诱导性提问、产品语言过早出现、题量过大。v2的关键设计:开场只做中性项目介绍,产品信息压到最后一节;第1题设分流闸,按对方当前是否带队走教练线或回溯线;复合问题拆成短主问加括号追问,让访谈员按接话情况灵活展开;两个设计概念(低介入干预、分级数据共享)配五步评估流程。核心原则:问具体的过去行为,不问观点和未来打算。之后补写第五题,问青少年运动员发育期的睡眠需求和训练-休息配比如何动态调整。

**EN — v2 Interview Guide for an Athlete Interviewee**
July 9–14 — Wrote the v2 interview guide — 33 questions, 60 minutes — for a PE faculty member who competed as a national-games swimmer.

Methodology came first (The Mom Test, Portigal's Interviewing Users, the Critical Incident Technique) alongside published qualitative studies of athlete sleep; then a review of my own earlier draft and a teammate's parallel one, which shared the same flaws — leading questions, product language appearing too early, too many questions. The v2 design: the opening gives only a neutral project introduction, with product information held back to the final section; question 1 acts as a gate, routing to a coach line or a retrospective line depending on whether the interviewee currently trains a team; compound questions are split into a short main question with follow-up probes in parentheses, so the interviewer can expand flexibly on whatever the interviewee engages with; and the two design concepts (low-intrusion intervention, tiered data sharing) get a five-step evaluation protocol. The governing principle throughout: ask about specific past behavior, never opinions or future intentions. A fifth question was later added on how adolescent athletes' sleep needs and training-to-rest ratios should adjust dynamically through growth.
