# 非项目对话｜Vice versa

### 用户

> vice versa

### 助手

> I'd be happy to help explain "vice versa"!
>
> **Vice versa** is a Latin phrase that means "the other way around" or "conversely." It's used when you want to indicate that a relationship or statement works in both directions.
>
> **Examples:**
>
> 1. "I help my roommate with English, and vice versa" = I help my roommate with English, and my roommate helps me with English too.
>
> 2. "Students can learn from teachers, and vice versa" = Students can learn from teachers, and teachers can learn from students.
>
> 3. "You can convert dollars to yuan, and vice versa" = You can convert dollars to yuan, and you can also convert yuan to dollars.
>
> **In Chinese:** 
> Vice versa ≈ 反之亦然 or 反过来也一样
>
> **Pronunciation:** 
> - VY-suh VUR-suh (American English)
> - VY-see VUR-suh (British English)
>
> It's a handy phrase for avoiding repetition when expressing two-way relationships!
