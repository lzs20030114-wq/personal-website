> **Phin Vietnamese Coffee** (913 S Jackson St) - Open Mon/Wed-Fri 8am-3pm, Sat-Sun 8am-5pm, closed Tuesdays. The menu focuses on classic phin-brewed Vietnamese coffee with thoughtful ingredients like homemade condensed milk. They also have pandan waffles, flan topped with coffee shaved ice, and Vietnamese yogurt.
>
> **Hood Famous Cafe + Bar** (504 5th Ave S) - A Filipino bakery and cafe known for their OG Ube Cheesecake, serving all-day breakfast, rice bowls, house-made pastries, and a single origin Asian Pacific coffee program. It's the only coffee shop in the Seattle area to exclusively feature beans from the Asia-Pacific region.
>
> **Fuji Bakery** (526 S King St) - Open daily from 7:30am-5pm (4pm Sundays). A Japanese-French bakery with excellent pastries and coffee. Their Japanese-meets-French offerings include curry buns, green tea Danishes, and various croissants.
>
> **Zeitgeist Coffee** (171 S Jackson St, Pioneer Square) - This classic Seattle coffeehouse has operated since 1997 and features an industrial vibe with local art. Lonely Planet called it the spot where "you'll get your first (and possibly best) cup of Seattle coffee."
