# 待确认主题｜SVG是什么

### 用户

> svg是什么？

### 助手

> SVG(Scalable Vector Graphics)是可缩放矢量图形,一种基于 XML 的图像格式。
>
> 跟普通图片(JPG、PNG)最大的区别在于:JPG/PNG 是**位图**,由一个个像素点组成,放大会糊;SVG 是**矢量图**,用数学描述图形——比如"从坐标 (10,10) 到 (90,90) 画一条线"、"以某点为圆心画半径 50 的圆"——所以无论放大多少倍都清晰锐利。
>
> 一个简单的 SVG 长这样:
>
> ```xml
> <svg width="100" height="100">
>   <circle cx="50" cy="50" r="40" fill="red" />
> </svg>
> ```
>
> 这几行代码就画了一个红色的圆。
>
> 对你来说几个相关的点:
>
> 它本质是**代码写出来的图形**,这跟参数化设计的思路很像——你不是手工画图,而是用规则和坐标去定义形状。Grasshopper 里那套"用数据驱动几何"的逻辑,在 SVG 里也有共鸣,只不过 SVG 是二维、更轻量。
>
> 在网页里(也就是你做 treepractice 那类网站时)SVG 用得很多:图标、logo、数据可视化的图表、可交互的动画,都常用它。因为它是文本格式,可以直接写进 HTML,也能用 JavaScript 操控、用 CSS 改样式,还能做动画。文件通常很小。
>
> 学代码的路上你会反复碰到它,尤其是做前端或者数据可视化的时候。不用专门去"学 SVG",用到的时候顺手查标签就行——它的语法很直观。

### 助手

> I'll search for current data on this.
>
> The data here is thinner and more fragmented than you might hope, so let me be upfront about what exists and what doesn't.
>
> There is no single authoritative, current "SVG generation ranking" of mainstream models. What exists is a scattering of academic benchmarks and informal comparisons, each with limitations. Here's the picture:
>
> The most-discussed informal test is **Simon Willison's "pelican riding a bicycle" benchmark** — you prompt a model to generate an SVG of a pelican on a bike and eyeball the result. It's deliberately whimsical, not rigorous, but it became a de facto reference point in the LLM community. Building on it, Tom Gally ran 30 creative prompts (like "an octopus operating a pipe organ") against 9 frontier models in late 2025; browsing the whole collection gives a good overall indication of which models are best at SVG art. But neither produces a clean numerical leaderboard — they're qualitative, you look and judge.
>
> On the academic side, **SVGenius** (ZJU, 2025) is the most systematic framework. Its key findings are more useful than any single ranking: proprietary models significantly outperform open-source ones, all models degrade systematically as complexity increases, and reasoning-enhanced training is more effective than pure scaling, though style transfer remains the hardest capability. Other academic benchmarks exist (VGBench/VGen, SVGEditBench V2) but they tend to test older model generations.
>
> There are also newer internal/industry benchmarks — **QwenSVG** and **SVG-Bench** — that report scores as Elo-style ratings and evaluate front-end and visual code generation across build-from-scratch tasks, but these are vendor-internal and don't give a neutral cross-model ranking.
>
> So the honest answer: I can't hand you a "latest ranking, models 1 through 10." That clean object doesn't really exist in a trustworthy form. The robust takeaways are the structural ones — closed models beat open ones, reasoning-trained models do better, and everything gets worse as the drawing gets complex.
>
