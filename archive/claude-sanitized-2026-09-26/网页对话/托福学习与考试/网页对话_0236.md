# 托福学习与考试｜New TOEFL test duration

### 用户

> how long will the new toefl test take?

### 助手

> The new TOEFL (starting January 21, 2026) takes approximately 90 minutes, down from the previous 2 hours. Some sources say up to 85 minutes or 67-85 minutes depending on the adaptive routing.
>
> Key changes in the new format:
> - **Reading & Listening**: Now adaptive (adjusts difficulty based on your performance), 18-27 minutes each
> - **Speaking**: Shortened to approximately 8 minutes (from 16) with new task types (Listen and Repeat, Take an Interview)
> - **Writing**: ~23 minutes with new formats including emails and academic discussion posts
> - **Scores**: New 1-6 band scale aligned with CEFR, plus the traditional 0-120 score during the transition period
> - **Results**: Available within 72 hours instead of 4-8 days
>
